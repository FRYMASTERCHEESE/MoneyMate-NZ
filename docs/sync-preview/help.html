Help & support

Start budgeting

MoneyMate NZ has Home, Transactions, Budgets, Savings goals, Bills, Export & backup, and Account on Android and in your browser. You can use it in a guest local profile without signing in.

Sign in and recovery

Use Account to register with email/password, confirm your email code, sign in or request a password reset. Keep access to a working, verified email address. Google sign-in is not available until the developer enables and tests the Google OAuth provider. A matching email address alone cannot guarantee that two separately created accounts share records.

Sync safely between devices

Sign into the same verified MoneyMate NZ account on Android and laptop. Open Account and choose Enable secure cloud sync. This asks permission before uploading sensitive financial records to Supabase. Once enabled on a device, the app checks the cloud after local changes and when reopened. Keep the device online for the change to upload. Offline records stay saved locally and upload on a later retry if the cloud has not changed.

If the cloud copy and device copy changed independently, the app stops rather than deleting either one. Export the full JSON backup on each device before selecting a copy to restore. There is no automatic merging of conflicting records in this release. Account identity persists independently of locally cached records, but no device or hosting service can guarantee data is never lost: keep a second backup.

Bills and recurring entries

Bills can be marked as paid to create a local expense. This does not send actual bank payments. Scheduled recurring entries are generated when you reopen the app or site and may require a cloud sync afterward.

Back up and restore

Export & backup saves full JSON backups compatible with Android and the website, or a CSV of transactions. Restore replaces that profile's local records, so back up first. Clearing browser storage and uninstalling the app can remove local copies.

Support

See the privacy policy and account deletion page. For a public technical issue that contains no personal data, use GitHub Issues. A private support channel must be added before public release.
