Delete your MoneyMate NZ account

You can delete your account here without reinstalling the app. Deletion permanently removes your Supabase account, its authentication identity and any optional online financial snapshot associated with the same user ID. It cannot be undone.

MoneyMate NZ only uploads financial records when you explicitly enable cloud sync. Account deletion also removes that synced server copy. To remove local records on this device, use Account → Delete account permanently in the app. After web deletion, clear MoneyMate NZ’s app storage on each device. Delete exported CSV/JSON files separately. The original local profile is preserved by in-app account deletion.

Supabase may retain limited security logs and backup copies under its retention rules. Google advertising data is governed by Google’s policy. Read our privacy policy.
