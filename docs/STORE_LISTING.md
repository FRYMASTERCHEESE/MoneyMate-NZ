# MoneyMate NZ — Google Play listing draft

**App title:** MoneyMate NZ: Budget Tracker

**Short description (under 80 characters):** Track NZD spending, budgets, savings goals and bills offline.

**Full description:**

MoneyMate NZ helps you manage your everyday money in New Zealand dollars.

Track your income and expenses in a simple transaction history, see your monthly balance, and discover where your money goes with easy category spending charts.

Set monthly spending budgets to help you stay on track. Create savings goals, add contributions and monitor your progress. Keep an organised list of upcoming bills and due dates.

Use weekly, fortnightly or monthly repeating transactions to automatically add records when you open the app. Export transactions to CSV, save and restore your complete records with a JSON backup, or import NZD bank transactions manually from CSV. Experimental PDF statement imports offer local text recognition with a mandatory review of extracted figures; compatibility varies by bank and document.

MoneyMate NZ works offline for manual budgeting without creating an account or paying a banking-data provider. Your records are stored locally by default. Signed-in users may choose secure cloud syncing for their private account using Supabase. You can download full JSON backups and CSV exports.

The free app is supported by advertising provided through Google AdMob, subject to privacy/consent choices.

**Feature highlights:**
- All amounts shown in NZ dollars
- Offline income and expense tracking
- Monthly budget category limits
- Savings goals and progress
- Bills and future due dates list
- Repeating income and expenses
- Spending category bars
- CSV export and JSON backup/restore
- Free manual CSV and experimental PDF bank statement import (NZD only)\n- Optional secure signed-in sync\n- No account required for offline manual budgeting

**Privacy:** MoneyMate NZ does not require your banking credentials and does not connect to your bank automatically. If you enable optional cloud sync, your financial records are uploaded to the account service in Sydney, Australia. Google authentication and advertising services may process account, device and advertising-related data. Review our privacy policy for details.

**Important:** MoneyMate NZ is a personal tracking tool, not a bank, financial adviser, or payment service.
