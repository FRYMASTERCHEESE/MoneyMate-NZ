Privacy Policy — MoneyMate NZ

Last updated: 12 October 2026.

What the app stores

MoneyMate NZ stores transaction amounts, dates, categories, notes, budgets, savings goals, bills and repeating transactions in a local database on your Android device. The app has no bank connection and does not require an account. The developer does not operate a server receiving your financial entries.

Export and backup

You can choose to export a CSV of transactions or a JSON backup of your data through Android's file picker. These exported files may include sensitive financial details; their location and any further sharing are under your control. Uninstalling or clearing app data deletes locally stored information unless you saved a separate backup.

Advertising and consent

This free app integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Advertising services may collect and process data such as app/device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic information in accordance with Google's policies. MoneyMate NZ requests advertising consent where required and offers an advertising privacy choices entry. The early-access Android build uses Google's demonstration ad identifiers, not production advertising identifiers. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Optional website theme refresh

MoneyMate NZ 0.2.1 and later checks a public, non-executable theme file hosted by GitHub Pages when opened or returned to. This request may disclose ordinary connection information such as your IP address to the web host. No financial entries, budgets, savings or bills are sent as part of this request. If offline, the app keeps using its last valid stored theme.

How your information is used

Locally stored financial records are used to display balances, budgets, bills and savings progress. They are not sent to the developer's servers. Advertising SDK data processing is separate from your local financial records.

Security and retention

The app uses Android's app-private SQLite storage. Access to exported backups is governed by the destination you select. Records remain on the device until you delete them or clear/uninstall the app.

Children

MoneyMate NZ is designed as a general-purpose personal budgeting tool, not directed at children.

Questions

For now, use the project's public GitHub issue tracker to report a technical problem. Before Google Play publication, the developer must provide a valid support email address and confirm this policy reflects the final production advertising implementation.
