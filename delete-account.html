Delete your MoneyMate NZ account

You can delete your account here without reinstalling the app. Deletion permanently removes your Supabase account and its authentication identity. It cannot be undone.

If you enable cloud sync, financial records are stored in Supabase as well as on your devices. Account deletion does not automatically erase local copies on other devices. Clear MoneyMate NZ’s app or browser storage on each device and delete exported CSV/JSON files separately. In-app account deletion retains the original local profile; it is not a device-storage wipe.

Supabase may retain limited security logs and backup copies under its retention rules. Google advertising data is governed by Google’s policy. Read our privacy policy.

Need help with account deletion? Email moneymatenzapp@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, verification codes or bank statements.
